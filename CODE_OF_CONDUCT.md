# Community conduct

Be rigorous about ideas and respectful toward people.

- Critique claims, evidence, interfaces, and reproducible behavior—not a student's ability, identity, institution, nationality, or advisor relationship.
- Never pressure contributors to reveal unpublished results or identify an advisor, student, laboratory, or social-media author.
- Use synthetic reproductions for public debugging.
- Treat disagreement about methodology as a request for evidence, not a license for ridicule or harassment.
- Maintainers may edit or remove content that creates privacy, safety, or identification risk.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. This short policy may be replaced by a fuller community standard if the contributor base grows.
