# Meeting Intents

Choose one primary intent. Add a secondary intent only when both materially affect the meeting.

| Intent | Lead with | Required decision shape | Usually move to backup |
|---|---|---|---|
| `progress` | what changed in the research state | confirm interpretation or next priority | routine activity and unchanged setup |
| `troubleshooting` | symptom, evidence, attempted exclusions, remaining hypotheses | select the most discriminating next check | full project history and unrelated successes |
| `decision` | choice, constraints, evidence, trade-offs | choose, defer, or request missing evidence | implementation detail that does not change the choice |
| `proposal` | question, rationale, feasibility evidence, largest unknown | approve direction or request a pilot/control | broad literature inventory |
| `mixed` | the highest-value decision, then only supporting secondary material | make the dependency between intents explicit | duplicated background for each subtopic |

Do not infer `decision` merely because next steps exist. A decision meeting requires a real choice, priority, go/no-go, or resource allocation question. Do not invent alternatives to make the meeting look decision-oriented.

For a sparse week, one anomaly or blocked action may be the entire meeting. A short meeting package is valid.
